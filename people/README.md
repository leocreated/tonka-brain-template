# people

Optional notes about people you work with, kept only when they help agents work with you (roles, how to reach them, preferences they have shared, ongoing threads).

- One person per file, named by a slug: `alex-rivera.md`.
- Write what you would be comfortable with that person reading. Skip anything sensitive (health, finances, family details) unless it is truly needed and you chose to record it.
- `overview.md` may hold a short summary written during setup.

New file: `node system/bin/brain.mjs new person "Full Name"`. Format: system/docs/record-format.md.
